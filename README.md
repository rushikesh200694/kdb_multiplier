# KBD Multiplier Dhule - E-Commerce Platform

Welcome to the **KBD Multiplier Dhule** platform, a comprehensive e-commerce website designed for selling farm products, Ayurvedic medicines, soaps, and other agricultural products.

## 📖 Project Overview
This platform bridges the gap between agricultural producers and consumers. It allows customers to easily browse high-quality farm products, read reviews, and explore company farm visits. It also provides an intuitive administrative panel for store owners to manage their inventory, orders, and customer interactions seamlessly.

## ✨ Key Features

### For Customers
* **Product Catalog:** Browse a diverse range of agricultural and organic products with detailed descriptions and dynamic pricing based on quantity or weight.
* **Farm Visits:** Explore organized company visits and demonstrations across Maharashtra, complete with photo galleries.
* **Shopping Cart & Checkout:** Seamless shopping experience with a fully functional cart and a streamlined checkout process.
* **Customer Reviews:** Read and submit reviews, including photos of the products and experiences.
* **Responsive Design:** Optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewing.

### For Administrators
* **Dashboard Analytics:** Get an overview of total products, visits, reviews, and pending orders.
* **Product Management:** Add, edit, or remove products and set their dynamic pricing.
* **Visit Management:** Schedule farm visits and manage their dedicated image galleries.
* **Order Management:** Track customer orders and update their fulfillment status.
* **Review Moderation:** Approve, reject, or delete customer reviews to maintain quality.
